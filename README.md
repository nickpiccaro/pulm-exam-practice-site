# Pulmonary Physiology Exam Practice

Unofficial, student-made practice exam for the pulmonary block of MEDC 30504 (Cell & Organ Physiology).

- 212 multiple-choice questions: every Question of the Day (33) and retired 2025 exam item (27), mixed in with 152 original NBME-style questions in the same style, across 9 topics: lung volumes & statics, dynamics & airway resistance, ventilation & the alveolar gas equation, diffusion, O₂/CO₂ transport, V/Q & pulmonary circulation, acid–base, control of ventilation & exercise, PFT interpretation
- 67 questions with built-in graphs: course-style P–V curves with respiratory-muscle lines (TLC/RV/FRC), flow–volume loops, spirograms, two-alveolus models, HbO₂ and O₂-content curves, capillary transit, CO₂ response, V/Q by lung height, PVR, Fowler washout, breathing patterns, surfactant hysteresis, CPET
- Full test or custom short tests (any length, any topics, unseen / missed / flagged pools, course-only or original-only), balanced across subtopics
- Explanations for every option shown right after you answer
- Live strengths/weaknesses report available mid-test; overall report across all tests
- Progress saves automatically in your browser only (export/import to move between computers)

Not affiliated with or reviewed by course faculty. AI-assisted; verify against lecture materials.

Static site, no build step: `index.html`, `fig.js` + `fig2.js` (figure renderers) and `q-*.js` (question bank; `q-qod.js` and `q-retired.js` hold the course items).
