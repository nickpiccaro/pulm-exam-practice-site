# Pulmonary Physiology Exam Practice

Unofficial, student-made practice exam for the pulmonary block of MEDC 30504 (Cell & Organ Physiology).

- 110 original NBME-style multiple-choice questions, written in the style of the retired 2025 pulmonary exam items and Questions of the Day, across 9 topics: lung volumes & statics, dynamics & airway resistance, ventilation & the alveolar gas equation, diffusion, O₂/CO₂ transport, V/Q & pulmonary circulation, acid–base, control of ventilation & exercise, PFT interpretation
- Built-in figures: pressure–volume curves, flow–volume loops, spirograms, HbO₂ curves, tidal-breath pressures, FEV₁ curves, CPET
- Full test or custom short tests (any length, any topics, unseen / missed / flagged pools), balanced across subtopics
- Explanations for every option shown right after you answer
- Live strengths/weaknesses report available mid-test; overall report across all tests
- Progress saves automatically in your browser only (export/import to move between computers)

Not affiliated with or reviewed by course faculty. AI-assisted; verify against lecture materials.

Static site, no build step: `index.html`, `fig.js` (figure renderer) and `q-*.js` (question bank).
